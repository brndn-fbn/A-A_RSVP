Our Story

A story of the right kind of love.

“Every cup tells a story.”

— Coffee Project

On March 13, 2019, a simple wave led to a coffee date, daily conversations, and eventually, deep talks about the future. We learned each other’s secrets that no one else knew. A crush turned into the deepest love. Uncertainty turned into assurance. Fear turned into peace.

Over the years, we’ve built a relationship grounded in faith and respect. We brought out the best in each other. We’ve grown hand in hand, learning to understand each other’s flaws and going through every chapter filled with love.

The joys and tears strengthened us, leading us to choose each other every day, until we finally said, “Yes,” to forever.

With God and you as our witnesses, we are ready to close this chapter and turn the page to whatever comes next.

This is not a perfect love story. It is not the kind of romance you see on TV. It is simply our story, a story of the right kind of love.
